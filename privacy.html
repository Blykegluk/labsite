Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

LAB ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our websites and services, including those of our subsidiaries.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: name and email address when you reach out to us.
  • Usage data: how you interact with our websites, including pages visited and device information.
  • Service-specific data: our subsidiaries may collect additional data as described in their respective privacy policies.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Operate and improve our websites.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely with encryption at rest and in transit. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data in compliance with GDPR and applicable French data protection regulations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data may be shared between LAB and its subsidiaries only as necessary to provide services you have requested.

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data.
  • Data portability.
  • Lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés).

Cookies

Our websites may use essential cookies to ensure proper functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
